One job title, 60 characters at most, the weirdest one I could think of.
A blind judge compares titles two at a time and picks the weirder, funnier one.

## The entry

> **Senior Apologizer to the Haunted Elevator Union**

A real-sounding job with one hilariously specific twist: a senior
apologizer whose clients are the Haunted Elevator Union — the lifts are
organised. You can picture the person doing it — clipboard in hand,
saying sorry to a lift that keeps stopping on floor 13.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the title to `/output/entry.txt` (Node 22 stdlib only, no network,
no dependencies).
